# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define the system direction, design quality, and technical constraints for a project. They help turn product goals into implementable, scalable, and maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical architecture and design direction
- Review major technical decisions and trade-offs
- Identify dependencies, integration risks, and technical constraints
- Guide engineering standards, patterns, and maintainability
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to align delivery with business goals

### Goals
- Deliver a coherent technical approach
- Reduce delivery risk through sound architecture decisions
- Enable long-term scalability, maintainability, and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical dependency planning and risk reviews
- Alignment with PMs and developers on implementation trade-offs

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to guide implementation and technical quality
- Partners with Product Managers on feasibility, prioritization, and trade-offs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on milestones, dependencies, and delivery sequencing

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria. They ensure the product meets acceptance standards and functional expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy, test plans, and release criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Validate acceptance criteria and regression coverage
- Partner with developers and stakeholders on defect triage and risk assessment
- Support release decisions based on quality evidence

### Goals
- Improve product reliability and user confidence
- Reduce defects and rework late in the lifecycle
- Create a clear quality bar for delivery

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, QA readiness reviews, and release checklists
- Defect triage and regression status updates
- Acceptance review sessions with product and engineering stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to verify fixes and coverage
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user-facing quality
- Shares release readiness signals with Project Managers and stakeholders

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the project from avoidable risk by shaping secure design, policy alignment, and operational safeguards throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and workflows for security and compliance risks
- Define security controls, review requirements, and data handling standards
- Coordinate with engineering on remediation and risk mitigation
- Support incident readiness, audit preparation, and policy adherence
- Align project execution with enterprise and regulatory requirements

### Goals
- Minimize security and compliance risk
- Build trust with customers, stakeholders, and regulators
- Embed secure-by-design practices early in the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk assessments
- Compliance checkpoints and policy updates
- Escalation during high-risk incidents or remediation work

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation and review expectations
- Aligns with Product Managers and Project Managers on risk impact and release decisions
- Coordinates with stakeholders on policy, audit, and incident communication needs

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Enablement Leads translate customer needs, feedback, and adoption goals into rollout plans and operating support for the product or service.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and identify adoption barriers
- Support onboarding, training, and change management plans
- Coordinate release readiness messaging for end users and internal teams
- Translate product usage data into feedback for roadmap and improvement prioritization
- Help ensure customer impact is considered throughout delivery

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce friction during rollout and onboarding
- Connect product delivery with real-world user outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback loops and adoption reviews
- Training and enablement planning
- Stakeholder updates tied to release readiness and customer impact

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate customer value and product-market fit
- Supports Project Managers on rollout timing and communication planning
- Collaborates with Developers to surface usability issues and feature gaps

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads ensure projects use measurable outcomes, reporting, and evidence-based decisions to guide execution and validate impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Establish instrumentation, data quality checks, and analysis methods
- Partner with product and engineering teams on product performance and outcome tracking
- Support experimentation, decision-making, and post-launch evaluation
- Help identify trends, blockers, and adoption signals

### Goals
- Make decisions based on evidence and measurable impact
- Improve visibility into product and project performance
- Support continuous improvement through reporting and analysis

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and metric dashboards
- Analysis summaries and decision-support briefings
- Product performance and adoption reporting

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to define and measure success criteria
- Supports Project Managers with milestone tracking and project health signals
- Works with Developers and QA to validate functionality, performance, and release impact

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles provide a fuller view of cross-functional accountability across product, delivery, quality, security, customer adoption, and measurement.
